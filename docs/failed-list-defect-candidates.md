# Failed List: application defect candidates

candidates for the product team to confirm; the automation does not work around them.
Tests that cover them are marked `@known-bug` and use `test.fail()`, so they pass while the
defect exists and fail loudly once it is fixed (remove the `test.fail()` then).

## Observations (not defects until a business owner says so)

- **Direct Sale visibility.** Bids are necessary (all 1,068 rows with the button have bids, a customer and a highest price above 0) but not sufficient: 49 rows with bids have no button, 47 of them auctions that ended in 2021. Row 24681 has a customer and a highest price but 0 bids, and no button. The rule needs a developer to confirm. The server still returns the Direct Sale form for rows without the button; whether it refuses the POST is unverified.
- **Direct Sale price boxes** drop `.` and `-` while typing ("10.5" becomes "105", "-1" becomes "1"). Supplier Price accepts letters and then reports "must be an integer".
- **Add to Auction selection.** The row checkbox is disabled for some rows: every row of suppliers 1927, 5136 and 7601, and 12 of 20 rows of supplier 141. The rule is unknown. When a supplier has no live or awaiting auction the picker says "Not found a live or awaiting auctions!".
- **Validate-only requests.** Typing in the Direct Sale form, pressing Suspend with no reason, and the Add to Auction and upload forms send POSTs carrying `ajax=<form id>`, which only return validation messages. They are counted separately from real submissions in the page objects.
- **XLS export** declares one empty trailing column and a blank row that the CSV and XLSX exports do not have.
- **Failed Reason "Total Registered"** is empty in the export.
- **Starting Price filter** removes letters and minus signs while typing and refuses a decimal with "Starting Price must be an integer." before searching.
